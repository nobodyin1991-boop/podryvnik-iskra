Iskra Blast

Iskra the courier robot is clearing an abandoned station. Blow up crates, find the upgrade and the hatch hidden under them, drive out every tenant and leave before time runs out.

Online duel

A match to three wins. You start in opposite corners; bombs, range and one detonator per half of the map lie under the crates. Slimes, beetles and spinners roam the map. Whoever blows up the opponent takes the round, and every round is on a new map.

Whoever creates the duel picks the map. They land on the map at once; the game stays paused and shows a link. The other player opens the link or types the code, and the pause lifts. The host must keep the page open: the game runs on their device.

How to play

  • Phone: the stick sits at the bottom right. Grab it and pull: the knob stretches after your thumb and keeps working even when the thumb goes far past the edge.
  • Bomb and Blast are in the centre, next to the stick. Iskra drives around corners by herself.
  • Keyboard: ←↑↓→ or WASD to move, Space bomb, X detonator, P pause.
  • Each bay hides one upgrade: +1 blast range or +1 bomb. There is a single detonator in the whole game: it is hidden in bay 06 and stays with you for good. Blasting an open hatch lets new tenants out.
  • You have three lives, and every fifth bay gives one more. When the lives run out everything resets and the route starts from bay one. From the Foundry on, maps are one and a half times wider.
